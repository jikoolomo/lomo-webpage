SECTION 05 / THE OBSERVER

I grow distant landscapes slowly in one record.

I used to be afraid of water.
Now I'm a diver.

I was not always free to travel, and I did not always know how to build things. I lived in unfamiliar cities, entered the water, crossed borders, and recorded what happened through photographs and writing.

At some point, recording was no longer enough, so I began building tools. Jikoo On is not a finished life; it is an ongoing experiment in how to live better on Earth.

LOMO HOUSE / A FUTURE SHELTER

A house where
distant climates stay together

LOMO House imagines contrasting environments met while traveling under one roof—a terrarium house still in the future.

The humidity of the sea, the light of the desert, the shadow of the forest, and the slow time of moss are woven into one spatial experience. Like a way of living on Earth beyond borders, LOMO House is a sketch of how different conditions can form a relationship.
