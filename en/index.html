LOMO HOUSE / A FUTURE SHELTER

A house where
distant climates stay together

LOMO House imagines contrasting environments met while traveling under one roof—a terrarium house still in the future.

The humidity of the sea, the light of the desert, the shadow of the forest, and the slow time of moss are woven into one spatial experience. Like a way of living on Earth beyond borders, LOMO House is a sketch of how different conditions can form a relationship.
